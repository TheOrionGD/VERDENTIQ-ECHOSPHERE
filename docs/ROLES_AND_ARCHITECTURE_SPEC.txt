VERDANTIQ ECOSPHERE - 3-TIER ARCHITECTURE & 8 SYSTEM ROLES SPECIFICATION
================================================================================

1. ARCHITECTURAL OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The VerdantIQ Ecosphere is built around a modern 3-Tier Enterprise Microservices 
Ecosystem designed for scalable sustainability intelligence:

A. CLIENT SERVICE (VerdantIQCLIENT):
   - Tech Stack: Next.js 15 (React 19), Tailwind & Vanilla CSS, Electron Desktop Shell,
     Capacitor Android Native Bridge.
   - Purpose: Multi-tenant UI/UX layer rendering role-tailored dashboards, dynamic 
     lazy-loaded chart bundles (Recharts, Pareto optimization curves, telemetry),
     real-time Server-Sent Events (SSE), and offline-tolerant state management.

B. SPRING BOOT BACKEND SERVICES (VerdantIQServices):
   - Tech Stack: Spring Boot API Gateway (sb-gateway), Eureka Service Registry,
     Spring Security with JWT & RBAC, Spring Data MongoDB / PostgreSQL, Kafka event buses.
   - Purpose: Enterprise orchestration, token authentication, cryptographically verified
     audit hash generation, tenant-level database isolation, rate limiting, and 
     secure request routing.

C. MACHINE LEARNING SERVICE (VerdantIQML):
   - Tech Stack: Python, FastAPI, PyTorch / Scikit-Learn, Pandas, NumPy.
   - Purpose: Predictive energy load forecasting, HVAC multi-objective optimization,
     Pareto frontier calculations, anomaly detection, model drift monitoring, and 
     automated pipeline retraining.


2. THE 8 SYSTEM ROLES: DETAILS, INPUTS & OUTPUTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ROLE 1: PLATFORM ADMIN (admin)
- Tier / Hierarchy: Tier 1 (Global System Level)
- Focus: Global Governance, System Health, Multi-tenant Configuration
- Inputs:
  * RBAC assignments and permission grants
  * Global tenant registrations & API gateway routing rules
  * Third-party system integrations & security parameter configurations
- Outputs:
  * Global platform telemetry (CPU, memory, latency, microservices health)
  * Cross-tenant immutable audit logs and security event alerts
  * Global platform uptime reports and tenant subscription metrics

ROLE 2: ML OPS ADMIN (mlops)
- Tier / Hierarchy: Tier 2 (AI Infrastructure Level)
- Focus: Machine Learning Pipelines, Model Registry, Inference Optimization
- Inputs:
  * Hyperparameter configurations & model solver optimization weights
  * Trigger commands for retraining pipelines
  * Dataset split parameters and drift detection thresholds
- Outputs:
  * Model drift indicators & inference latency graphs
  * Multi-objective Pareto frontier charts (Cost vs. Carbon vs. Comfort)
  * Model registry versions, deployment canary health metrics

ROLE 3: AUDITOR / RESEARCHER (audit)
- Tier / Hierarchy: Tier 3 (Independent Compliance Level)
- Focus: Independent Compliance, Cryptographic Integrity, Carbon Protocol Verification
- Inputs:
  * Date range audit queries & compliance rule filters
  * Verification requests on carbon reduction claims
  * Scientific research extraction parameters
- Outputs:
  * Cryptographically sealed, read-only immutable audit logs
  * Certified carbon accounting compliance certificates
  * Anonymized raw telemetry exports for peer review and academic verification

ROLE 4: REGIONAL ADMIN (region)
- Tier / Hierarchy: Tier 4 (Regional District Authority)
- Focus: Regional Grids, District Energy Targets, Cross-Campus Benchmarks
- Inputs:
  * District carbon reduction quotas and peak demand mandates
  * Emergency demand-response curtailment directives
  * Regional baseline energy caps
- Outputs:
  * Multi-campus energy benchmarks and regional grid carbon intensity indexes
  * District aggregated load profiles & emissions distribution heatmaps
  * Regional compliance and environmental risk alerts

ROLE 5: INSTITUTION ADMIN (institution)
- Tier / Hierarchy: Tier 5 (Academic & Campus Estate Oversight)
- Focus: Campus ESG Reporting, Renewable Energy Procurement, Departmental Quotas
- Inputs:
  * Green capital allocation budgets and clean energy investments
  * Campus-wide carbon reduction targets & facility schedule policies
  * Department moderator role authorizations
- Outputs:
  * Comprehensive campus ESG scorecard & Scope 1, 2, 3 carbon reports
  * Renewable energy procurement efficiency breakdowns
  * Cross-department sustainability rankings and resource allocations

ROLE 6: DEPARTMENT MODERATOR (dept)
- Tier / Hierarchy: Tier 6 (Department / Facility Oversight)
- Focus: Local HVAC Schedules, Energy Anomaly Triage, Student Verification
- Inputs:
  * Lab, classroom, and facility HVAC operating hours
  * Approvals / rejections of student eco-action proof submissions
  * Local carbon budget allocations
- Outputs:
  * Department real-time load curves and power consumption profiles
  * Student EcoPoint verification queues and participation analytics
  * Localized anomaly alerts (equipment left running overnight, abnormal surges)

ROLE 7: STUDENT (student)
- Tier / Hierarchy: Tier 7 (Campus Learner & Participant)
- Focus: Campus Eco-Challenges, Energy Awareness, Cohort Engagement
- Inputs:
  * Photo/timestamp submissions for lab shutdowns & green actions
  * Enrollment in campus sustainability initiatives and hackathons
  * Dorm energy digital twin optimization preferences
- Outputs:
  * Individual and team EcoPoint scores & reward badges
  * Cohort leaderboard positions and campus ranking
  * Real-time campus & dorm digital twin energy metrics

ROLE 8: STANDARD USER / HOUSEHOLD (user)
- Tier / Hierarchy: Tier 8 (Residential Citizen Level)
- Focus: Smart Home Energy Management, Residential Footprint Optimization
- Inputs:
  * Smart home device connections & appliance scheduling constraints
  * Household eco-saving goals and energy budget thresholds
- Outputs:
  * Household 7-day load forecasts and cost projection charts
  * Actionable energy reduction recommendations & automated schedule suggestions
  * Regional benchmark comparisons (household vs. district average)


3. INTERCONNECTION & DATA FLOW ARCHITECTURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

                                  [Platform Admin] (Tier 1)
                                             |
                      +----------------------+----------------------+
                      |                                             |

                                             |
                       +---------------------+---------------------+

       (Campus ESG & Infrastructure)                     (Residential Households)
                       |
         [Department Moderator] (Tier 6)
       (Lab Schedules & Verification)
                       |
               [Student] (Tier 7)
        (Eco Challenges & Proof Submissions)


A. Top-Down Policy & Model Distribution:
   - Admin sets security, tenancy boundaries, and system governance.
   - MLOps designs and publishes optimized models into the Model Registry.
   - Regional Admin defines regional targets and grid carbon limits.
   - Institutions inherit regional targets and translate them into campus policies.
   - Department Moderators enforce campus policies on buildings and labs.
   - Households access regional energy efficiency recommendations.

B. Bottom-Up Telemetry, Proof Verification & Compliance:
   - Students submit proof of sustainable actions (e.g., equipment shutdowns).
   - Department Moderators review and approve student proofs, granting EcoPoints.
   - Aggregated department savings feed into the Institution ESG Scorecard.
   - Multi-institution metrics roll up to Regional Admin district dashboards.
   - Auditors inspect cryptographic audit hashes across all tiers, verifying that
     reported carbon savings and compliance figures are untampered and mathematically valid.
